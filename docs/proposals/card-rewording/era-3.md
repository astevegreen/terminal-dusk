# Era 3 and future cards: rules text reworded now that points have a use

The 37 cards of `docs/design/cards/era-3.md` (31 Era 3 cards and the 6 future cards), in that file's order.
Wording rules, open points and their alternative wordings are in [README.md](README.md). Each entry gives today's
rules text (from the card's JSON) and the draft. **Open points** names the README open points the draft depends on;
the draft is written in their first alternative (Split-any, Turn-end-any, Tech-yes, Q28-A as today) and picks none
of them. The three cards that move Escalation (Annexation of Crimea, Full-Scale Invasion, Hypersonics on Parade)
also get a draft Design and Playable line. Nothing else in any card's effect changes. Each draft waits for the
owner's approval.

## Questions for the owner

"x" is what a -1 Ops swing costs the side over the rest of the turn: 0 if it spends nothing more this turn, 1 for
each later spend of 2 or more (a spend of 1 stays 1). **Gap** and **Own** are the scoring readings in README.md;
scoring is not decided. Every option uses steps that exist today.

1. **Annexation of Crimea and Full-Scale Invasion: README's templates cannot say when the raise happens.** Under
   P2 the move is the last step, after the `on turnEnd { spend lio 1 }` step. The raise happens when the card is
   played (the turn-end step only waits), but the sentence "Raise Escalation by 1." follows "At the end of this
   turn, LIO spends 1 point on actions." and can be read as happening at turn end. The drafts use README's
   wording as it is.
   - **T1, keep:** last step and README's wording. One rule everywhere; the reader must know a turn-end sentence
     only waits.
   - **T2, move the raise before the turn-end step:** `...; escalate +1; on turnEnd { spend lio 1 }`. The game
     does the same thing (the raise still comes after every spend made now); the sentences then read in the order
     things happen. But the rule becomes "after every spend made now", not "last step".
   - **T3, keep the order and reword:** _Now raise Escalation by 1._ This changes README's template, which is
     outside this task (X10a's file).
   - **Recommend T2:** same effect, and the text cannot be misread. Under Turn-end-any, whether LIO's turn-end
     point meets the track before or after the turn's own -1 is for R17 to settle; it does not change this choice.
2. **Tariff Shock (question 44): holding fire (SAE 1 at turn end) is never worse than retaliating (SAE 1 now and
   -1 Ops).** Only SAE's points depend on the answer, so Gap and Own give the same result.
   - **A, keep:** under Turn-end-any, no is always at least as good (the same 1 point, no x, and LIO cannot answer
     it this turn). Under Turn-end-place, yes buys the right to intervene or realign with the point now, at the
     cost x: a small choice.
   - **B, drop the penalty (question 44's second fix):** yes becomes `spend sae 1`. The choice is when SAE spends 1
     point: now, where it can shape SAE's later plays this turn but LIO can answer it, or at turn end, where LIO
     cannot. Cheapest change; retaliation no longer costs SAE anything.
